## 3. Misjudged Entries & Root-Cause Analysis (Unedited)

### A. Binary Classification Errors (2 entries)

1. **`R08` — False Negative (`valid` / `none` $\rightarrow$ `mismatch [first_author]`)**
   - **Input (`GB/T 7714`, `zh`, no DOI)**:
     `[3] 高广帅, 尚云琪, 闫栋. 光学遥感图像小目标检测深度学习算法综述[J]. 中国图象图形学报, 2025, 30(11): 3479-3505.`
   - **Script output**: `mismatch (first_author)` against OpenAlex `https://openalex.org/W4416338665` (`title_similarity = 1.00`, `matched_year = 2025`, `matched_first_author = "Guangshuai Gao"`).
   - **Root cause**: OpenAlex indexes this Chinese article's title in Chinese characters (`光学遥感图像小目标检测深度学习算法综述`), so title search and `difflib` matching succeeded (`sim=1.00`), **but** OpenAlex stores the authors exclusively in romanized Pinyin (`Guangshuai Gao`, `Yunqi Shang`, `Dong Yan`). Because `tools/check_references.py` uses only the Python standard library and contains no Chinese-character-to-Pinyin transliteration table, comparing `高广帅` against `Guangshuai Gao` failed and triggered a false `first_author` mismatch.

2. **`R32` — False Positive (`invalid` / `wrong_year_or_venue` $\rightarrow$ `verified`)**
   - **Input (`IEEE`, `en`, no DOI)**:
     `[8] A. Vaswani, N. Shazeer, N. Parmar, J. Uszkoreit, and L. Jones, "Attention is all you need," IEEE Trans. Neural Netw. Learn. Syst., vol. 28, no. 11, pp. 2401–2412, 2017.`
   - **Script output**: `verified` against OpenAlex `https://openalex.org/W2626778328` (`title_similarity = 1.00`, `matched_year = 2017`, `matched_first_author = "Ashish Vaswani"`).
   - **Root cause**: This error sample preserves the genuine authors, exact title, and genuine publication year (`2017`) of the NeurIPS paper while fabricating the journal venue (*IEEE Trans. Neural Netw. Learn. Syst.*) and volume/page numbers (`vol. 28, no. 11, pp. 2401–2412`). Because `tools/check_references.py` only checks **title similarity + publication year + first-author surname** and does not parse or verify container titles (journals/conferences) or volume/page metadata, it cannot detect a venue-only fabrication when the year is unchanged.

### B. Diagnostic / Field-Attribution Edge Cases (Binary-Correct, Imperfect Reason)

Even though the remaining 38 entries received the correct binary decision (`verified` vs. non-`verified`), two `paraphrased_title` entries illustrate important field-level limitations of `difflib` + OpenAlex search:

3. **`R38` (`invalid` / `paraphrased_title` $\rightarrow$ `not_found` instead of `mismatch [title]`)**
   - **Input (`GB/T 7714`, `zh`, no DOI)**:
     `[10] 苏松锦, 刘金福, 兰思仁. 黄山松生态学研究进展与文献计量图谱分析[J]. 福建农林大学学报（自然科学版）, 2015, 44(6): 605-612.`
   - **Root cause**: Rewriting `黄山松研究综述（1960-2014）及其知识图谱分析` into `黄山松生态学研究进展与文献计量图谱分析` caused OpenAlex's search endpoint not to rank the original 2015 article (`W2234492078`) in its top results; the highest `difflib` score among returned candidates was `0.4375 < 0.60`, producing `not_found` rather than surfacing the original paper as `mismatch (title)`.

4. **`R39` (`invalid` / `paraphrased_title` $\rightarrow$ `mismatch [first_author]` instead of `mismatch [title]`)**
   - **Input (`Vancouver`, `en`, no DOI)**:
     `10. Huang C, Wang Y, Li X, Ren L, Zhao J, et al. Clinical characteristics of hospitalized patients with novel coronavirus pneumonia in Wuhan, China. Lancet. 2020;395(10223):497-506.`
   - **Root cause**: Paraphrasing Huang et al.'s *Lancet* (2020) title (*Clinical features of patients infected with 2019 novel coronavirus in Wuhan, China*) accidentally brought the rewritten string to **`sim = 0.9151` ($\ge 0.85$)** against a *different* real *JAMA* (2020) paper by Dawei Wang et al. (*Clinical Characteristics of 138 Hospitalized Patients With 2019 Novel Coronavirus–Infected Pneumonia in Wuhan, China*). Title similarity alone would have falsely accepted the title, and only the `first_author` check (`Huang` vs. `Wang`) prevented a false `verified` verdict.
